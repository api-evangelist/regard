# Regard

> Regard is an AI Clinical Insights Platform for health systems. It reviews 100% of the EHR to recommend diagnoses and generate documentation at the point of care — earning revenue through better care, not queries.

## Main Pages

- [Home](https://regard.com/): Overview of Regard's AI-powered clinical diagnosis and documentation platform
- [Demo](https://regard.com/demo): Request a demo of the AI Clinical Insights Platform and see how Proactive Documentation closes the Clinical Insights Gap

## Solutions

- [Clinical Notes](https://regard.com/solutions/clinical-notes): Help clinicians write better notes faster — AI drafts documentation from the full chart before the encounter
- [Mid-Revenue Cycle](https://regard.com/solutions/mid-revenue-cycle): Proactive Documentation that improves coding accuracy and reduces queries
- [Population Health & HCC Capture](https://regard.com/solutions/hcc-capture): AI review of 100% of patient data to strengthen HCC capture and surface missed conditions
- [Clinical Screening](https://regard.com/solutions/screening): Full-chart review to surface diagnoses and care opportunities earlier
- [Close the HCC Gap](https://regard.com/hcc-gap): Surface missed diagnoses, improve risk adjustment capture, and protect population health revenue
